Understanding Your Spending Patterns
This report analyzes your spending patterns to help you understand your financial habits. Spending patterns reveal when you spend the most money, where you shop most frequently, and your preferred shopping days. Use these insights to make better financial decisions.
Key Insights to Look For:
  • Peak Spending Days: Days with the highest spending - often paydays, weekends, or specific events
  • Merchant Patterns: Most visited merchants and total spent at each location
  • Frequency vs Amount: High frequency with low amounts might indicate daily necessities
  • Optimization Opportunities: Areas where you can consolidate purchases or find alternatives
Weekday Spending Analysis

Merchant Frequency Analysis
This chart reveals your shopping habits by showing which merchants you visit most frequently and how much you spend at each location. High frequency with low amounts might indicate daily necessities, while high amounts might suggest luxury or impulse purchases.
Pattern Analysis Tips
How to Use This Information:
  • Identify Peak Spending Days: Look for days with the highest spending. Are these paydays, weekends, or specific events? Understanding your peak days can help you plan better and avoid overspending.
  • Analyze Merchant Patterns: Check which merchants you visit most frequently. High frequency with low amounts might indicate daily necessities, while high amounts might suggest luxury or impulse purchases.
  • Optimize Your Spending: Use pattern insights to make better financial decisions. Consider consolidating purchases, finding alternatives for frequent high-cost merchants, or adjusting your shopping schedule.
  • Compare Across Time Periods: Compare patterns across different time periods to see how your spending habits evolve and identify seasonal trends or lifestyle changes.
