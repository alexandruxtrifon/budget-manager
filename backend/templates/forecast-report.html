What is a Financial Forecast?

A forecast uses your past income and expense patterns to predict future trends. It helps you anticipate cash flow, spot potential shortfalls, and plan ahead with confidence.

  • Forecasts are based on your historical transactions and statistical models.
  • They show likely trends, not certainties—use them as a guide, not a guarantee.
  • Regularly updating your data improves forecast accuracy.

How to Read Your Forecast

  • Solid lines show your actual historical data. Dotted lines are the forecasted values for the next 30 days.
  • Look for trends: Are your expenses rising? Is your income stable?
  • Use the forecast to plan for upcoming bills, savings, or investments.
  • If you see a predicted shortfall, consider adjusting your spending or boosting your income.
  • The table shows the next 7 days of detailed forecasts to help with short-term planning.
